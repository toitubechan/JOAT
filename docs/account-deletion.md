# JOAT Account and Data Deletion

JOAT (Jack of All Trades) users can request deletion of their account and
associated personal data without having the app installed.

## Request deletion

Email [toitubechan@gmail.com](mailto:toitubechan@gmail.com?subject=JOAT%20account%20and%20data%20deletion%20request) from the address used for your JOAT account with
the subject **JOAT account and data deletion request**. State that you want
your JOAT account and associated data deleted. If you signed in with Google, use
the email address shown in JOAT. Do not send a password, verification code,
payment information, or identity documents in your first message.

If JOAT is installed, you can also open **Settings → Account & privacy → Request
account & data deletion** to prepare this email. The request is not sent until
you tap Send in your email app. We may ask you to verify account ownership before
processing the request. If you cannot access the original email address, contact
us at the same support address and explain that in your request.

We will complete verified deletion requests within **30 days after verifying
account ownership** and confirm the outcome by email. If limited records must
be retained for a legal, security, fraud-prevention, or financial obligation,
we will explain which records are retained and why.

## What the request covers

After verification, we will delete your Firebase Authentication account,
synced progress/preferences in Cloud Firestore, JOAT-linked person/events data
in PostHog, and customer data in RevenueCat, where those records exist, subject
to the limited retention described above.

Learning progress and preferences also have a copy on your phone. You can remove
that copy by clearing JOAT's app data in Android settings or uninstalling JOAT.
A support request cannot erase an offline phone's local storage remotely. If you
use more than one device, clear the app data on each device.

Deleting your JOAT account or uninstalling JOAT does not refund or erase the
one-time JOAT Pro purchase record held by Google Play. Contact us about JOAT Pro
access or use Google Play's purchase support for a refund request. Google Play
may keep its purchase record under its own billing and retention rules.

This request deletes your **JOAT account**, not your Google account or accounts
you use with other apps.

## Questions

Contact **toitubechan@gmail.com** about a request or its status.
